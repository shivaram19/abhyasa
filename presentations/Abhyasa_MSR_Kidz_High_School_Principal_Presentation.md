# The teacher sets the standard. The AI applies it at scale.

1. Teacher uploads the **answer key** and marking scheme.
2. Students submit answer scripts — handwritten or typed.
3. Abhyāsa AI evaluates against the teacher’s key.
4. Results and error patterns are ready in minutes.

```
Teach → Assess → Diagnose → Practice → Re-assess → Master
              ↑___________________________________|
```

## Appendix A: Anticipated Questions & Answers

**Q: Will the AI mark subjective answers correctly?**  
A: The teacher provides the answer key and marking scheme. The AI follows that scheme. Subjective judgment stays with the teacher for edge cases.

**Q: What if a teacher disagrees with the AI-generated worksheet?**  
A: The teacher can edit, replace, or reject any AI suggestion before it reaches students.

**Q: Can the principal see everything?**  
A: Yes. The principal has an approval dashboard for lesson plans and access to performance summaries.

**Q: Is student data safe?**  
A: Yes. Data is stored securely in India, encrypted at rest and in transit, and never used for ads or sold.

**Q: Do we need special devices?**  
A: Teachers can use existing laptops or tablets. Students can receive worksheets as printouts or PDFs.
